# sanborn-key-plan-1917 (backdrop)

Not a vector layer: a georeferenced scan, listed in `data/backdrops.json`.

- Source: Sanborn Map Co., key plan of volumes 2 and 4, "showing territory destroyed by fire, May 21st, 1917" (May 31, 1917), University of Georgia Libraries, Digital Library of Georgia ([dlg_sanb_atlanta-1917](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_sanb_atlanta-1917)). Public domain (published 1917). The image is `chapters/1917-hot-ash/art/sanborn-key-plan-1917.jpg`; its rights basis is in that chapter's sources.
- Georeferenced 2026-10-27 from eight street crossings read off the scan by eye and matched to the 1928 street layer: RMS 17 m, worst 23 m, about 2.3 m per pixel. The sheet's own scale is 1000 feet to the inch, and the street grid changed between 1917 and 1928, so treat features traced from it as good to a few tens of metres.
- Credit, if shown: "Sanborn Map Co. key plan, 1917, University of Georgia Libraries".
